# BUSINESS_RULES.md

## BarPro Premium Business Rules

## Project Mission

BarPro Premium exists to generate qualified leads for bar catering services.

The website is not a portfolio.
The website is not a blog.
The website is a sales and lead generation system.

Every design, content and technical decision should support lead generation.

---

## Business Priorities

Priority order:

1. Lead Generation
2. Conversion Rate
3. SEO Traffic
4. Trust Building
5. Performance
6. Design

If there is a conflict between visual design and conversion rate, conversion rate wins.

---

## Primary Conversion Goal

The primary conversion action is:

**"Calculate Event Cost"**

All important pages should encourage users to visit: `/calculator`

This is the most valuable page on the website.

---

## Secondary Conversion Goals

If the user is not ready to use the calculator:

Alternative actions:

* Request a consultation
* Leave contact details
* Request a commercial proposal
* Contact manager

---

## Calculator Rules

**Business Importance: CRITICAL**

The calculator must:

* Be visible from important pages
* Be easy to find
* Work on mobile devices
* Require minimal effort

Never:

* Hide calculator links
* Add unnecessary steps
* Increase friction
* Require registration

Every change should improve or preserve conversion rate.

---

## Lead Collection Rules

Required lead fields:

* Name
* Phone

Optional:

* Email
* Comment

Business rule:

**Short forms convert better.**

Do not increase form complexity without strong reason.

---

## CTA Rules

Every important page must contain at least one CTA.

Preferred CTA texts:

* Рассчитать стоимость
* Получить предложение
* Узнать стоимость мероприятия
* Получить консультацию

Avoid generic buttons:

* Подробнее
* Читать далее

Without a conversion-focused CTA nearby.

---

## Homepage Rules

Homepage goals:

1. Explain service
2. Build trust
3. Drive calculator usage

Homepage should include:

* Clear value proposition
* CTA above the fold
* Service overview
* Cases preview
* Testimonials
* Team preview
* Calculator CTA

Homepage must always contain a visible path to the calculator.

---

## Calculator Page Rules

Calculator page exists for one purpose: **Generate leads.**

Avoid:

* Excessive content
* Long text blocks
* Distractions

The calculator should remain the dominant element on the page.

---

## Cases Rules

Purpose: Build trust.

Every case should answer:

* What event was held
* How many guests attended
* What services were provided
* What result was achieved

Cases should demonstrate experience and professionalism.

Cases are sales assets, not blog posts.

---

## Team Rules

Purpose: Reduce trust barriers.

Visitors should see real people.

Team content should emphasize:

* Experience
* Professionalism
* Reliability

Stock photos are discouraged.

---

## Testimonials Rules

Purpose: Increase trust.

Testimonials should:

* Feel authentic
* Be easy to read
* Support conversion

Testimonials should appear near CTA sections when possible.

---

## SEO Rules

SEO exists to generate commercial traffic.

Priority keywords:

* bar catering
* cocktail catering
* mobile bar
* event bartender
* wedding bar
* corporate event bar

Traffic quality is more important than traffic volume.

---

## Landing Page Rules

Every landing page should contain:

1. Offer
2. Benefits
3. Cases
4. Testimonials
5. CTA
6. Contact option

Pages without a CTA are incomplete.

---

## Mobile Rules

Mobile visitors are high priority.

Every conversion action must be accessible on mobile.

Required:

* Sticky CTA button when appropriate
* Fast loading
* Large tap targets (min 48px)

Avoid:

* Small buttons
* Complex forms
* Hidden actions

---

## Content Rules

Content should focus on:

* Benefits
* Results
* Event experience

Not:

* Internal company details
* Technical information
* Industry jargon

Always answer: *"Why should the client choose us?"*

---

## Pricing Rules

Pricing should feel transparent.

The calculator should provide realistic estimates.

Avoid:

* Hidden pricing logic
* Unrealistic prices
* Misleading discounts

Trust is more important than aggressive pricing tactics.

---

## Trust Rules

Every important page should strengthen trust through:

* Cases
* Testimonials
* Team
* Photos
* Experience

Trust signals should be visible before asking for contact information.

---

## Design Rules

Design exists to support conversion.

Avoid changes that:

* Reduce readability
* Hide CTAs
* Increase scrolling to key actions
* Distract users from conversion goals

A visually impressive page that converts worse is considered a failure.

---

## AI Development Rules

When proposing changes, always ask:

1. Does this improve lead generation?
2. Does this improve conversion?
3. Does this improve trust?
4. Does this improve SEO?
5. Does this improve mobile UX?

If the answer is "no" to all five questions, the change is likely unnecessary.

---

## Release Approval Rules

A release cannot be approved if:

* Calculator is broken
* Lead submission is broken
* Email delivery is broken
* Mobile navigation is broken
* CTA visibility is reduced
* SEO metadata is removed

Business functionality always has priority over cosmetic improvements.
